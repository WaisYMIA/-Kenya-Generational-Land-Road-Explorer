4. Arbitrage, Registered Family Trust & Due Diligence Hub

Preserving multi-acre wealth over 15 to 20 years requires selecting the right legal vehicle for minor children, respecting KeNHA centerline road reserves, and completing a five-week physical and registry audit before releasing funds. Switch tabs below to review plot arbitrage spreads, compare legal structures, verify road setback widths, and track pre-purchase checks.

Recommended Structure: Incorporate a non-trading Registered Family Trust under the Trustees (Perpetual Succession) Act (Cap 164) within the statutory 60-day Registry window (KES 500 registration duty). Title vests directly in the trust's corporate name, bypassing court probate, preventing minor repudiation at age 18 under Section 49 of the Land Registration Act, and allowing delayed vesting (e.g., age 25 or postgraduate completion) overseen by an independent Enforcer.
